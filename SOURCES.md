# Sources for non-IBA cards

Drinks that are not on the IBA list but a working bar serves constantly. Specs follow the US bartending-school and bar-management standard rather than any single author's house recipe. Every entry cites what it was checked against and states where it departs.

## Pour standard

US standard pour: one shot = 1.5 oz (45 ml). Highball / single-mixer drink = one shot built over ice and filled with mixer, roughly 1:4 (Barnard College Bartending Course study guide; WebstaurantStore bar guide gives 1.5 oz as the 3-count standard for single-mixer cocktails and 2 oz as the 4-count for neat or rocks; TIPS gives 1.5 oz as the standard US shot). Up and stirred drinks = 2 oz base, matching the IBA Dry Martini (60 ml). Sours = 2 oz base, 3/4 oz citrus, 3/4 oz sweet (Williams College mixology course notes). Difford's Guide is cited as a secondary check; its 50-60 ml pours are UK measures and run heavy for a US bar.

- Barnard College Bartending Course study guide: https://barnard.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/bartending_training_course_-_study_guide.pdf
- WebstaurantStore, Measuring Liquor: Alcohol Pour Counts for Bars: https://www.webstaurantstore.com/blog/4162/how-to-measure-liquor.html
- TIPS (360training), How to Pour and Measure Drinks: https://www.gettips.com/blog/how-to-pour-and-measure-drinks
- Williams College mixology course notes (sour ratios): https://www.cs.williams.edu/~heeringa/classes/mixology/w12/notes/slides.1.pdf
- Difford's Guide, highball definition: https://www.diffordsguide.com/g/1086/highballs/what-is-a-highball

Popularity tiers: core = DI top 15 or a US on-premise volume staple (CGA by NIQ); common = DI 16-50 or a routine event-bar order; rare = everything else.

Drinks International ranking: Drinks International, 'The bestselling classic cocktails at the world's best bars 2026' (Cocktail Report 2026, published 2026-04-28). Poll of ~100 top bars in 30+ countries, each naming its ten bestselling classics. https://drinksint.com/news/fullstory.php/aid/12284/.html

US volume staples: CGA by NIQ, Cocktail Sales Tracker (bevindustry.com 2024-12-30; nielseniq.com 2025-06-24): Margarita first by volume at ~4x the next drink, then Mimosa, Mojito, Espresso Martini, Spritz, Old Fashioned, Martini, Daiquiri, Pina Colada.

## Bootleg

- Lunds & Byerlys, The Bootleg Cocktail: A Minnesota Tradition (2 : 2 : 2 oz): https://corporate.lundsandbyerlys.com/the-bootleg-cocktail-a-minnesota-tradition/
- Artful Living, Bootleg recipe: https://artfulliving.com/bootleg-minnesota-summer-cocktail-recipe/
- Deviation from reference: Equal parts per Lunds & Byerlys. Artful Living's mix uses frozen lemonade and limeade concentrate; either mix works. Folk drink, no canonical spec, and the 2 oz pour is the drink's own convention, not the house shot.
